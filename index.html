How to Use This Tool

A 5-step guide for Scott & Janell

1
Rate independently
Use the Scott's Ratings and Janell's Ratings tabs to switch between your two lists. Each person should rate items on their own — before comparing — to get honest, uninfluenced responses. For each characteristic, tap a number:
1 Nice to have 2 Prefer 3 Important 4 Must have
Tap the same number again to clear a rating. You can skip items that don't apply.
2
Add notes
Each row has a notes field on the right. Use it to add context — "needs to fit a 30-ft trailer" or "a must for the dogs." Notes show up in the analysis report and in the agent email, so they're worth filling in for your higher-rated items.
3
Add your own items
Scroll to the bottom of the rating list and use the Add Your Own field to add any characteristic not already in the list. Custom items are saved and rated the same way as the built-in ones.
4
Generate Analysis
Once both of you have rated, click Generate Analysis in the right panel. The report shows:
  • Shared Non-Negotiables — both rated 4 or 3+
  • Biggest Gaps — items 3 points apart; start your conversation here
  • Worth Discussing — 2-point gaps; probably resolvable
  • Aligned — within 1 point of each other
  • A Complete Ratings Table with every rated item and all notes
5
Export & Share
After running analysis, two export options appear:
  • Export Analysis as PDF — opens the browser print dialog. Choose "Save as PDF" to save the full report including all notes. Best used in Chrome.
  • Generate Agent Email — creates a clean, agent-facing summary (no disagreement framing) sorted into Must-Haves, Strong Preferences, and Nice to Haves. Copy to clipboard or open directly in your mail app.
Saving Your Work
Your ratings are auto-saved in this browser as you go — look for the "✓ Auto-saved" indicator in the top-right of the analysis panel. If you close and reopen the file in the same browser, everything will be right where you left it.

To share between devices or with Janell on a separate machine, use Save File to download a .json snapshot, and Load File to restore it. Loading a file also updates local auto-save immediately.
Tips
  • The most useful conversations come from the Biggest Gaps section — not the disagreements themselves, but understanding why you rated something differently.
  • If Janell is rating on a separate device, have her fill out her ratings, Save File, and send you the .json. Load it on your machine, add your ratings, and run the analysis.
  • Use the Reset button to start completely fresh. It clears all ratings, notes, custom items, and local auto-save after confirmation.
